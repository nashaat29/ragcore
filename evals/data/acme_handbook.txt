Acme Corporation Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

Section 4: Working Location and Remote Work

Acme operates a hybrid model. Employees assigned to an office are expected to be physically
present in the office at least three days per week: Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are
core collaboration days. Monday and Friday are flexible and may be worked remotely from
within the country of employment. Fully remote roles exist for positions marked
"distributed" in the job catalog, and those employees may work from anywhere in their
contracted country, but must be reachable during their agreed core hours of 10:00 to 16:00
local time. Managers may approve additional remote days for short periods such as home
repairs or family obligations without a formal request.

Section 5: Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off per month of service, which totals
18 days per year. Accrual begins on the first day of employment and vests monthly.
Part-time employees accrue proportionally to their scheduled hours. Up to five unused days
may be carried into the next calendar year; anything above five is forfeited unless a
manager approves a written exception before December 15. PTO requests of three consecutive
days or more require approval two weeks in advance. Sick leave is tracked separately at
six days per year and does not require advance notice for the first two days.

Section 6: Expense Reporting

All business expenses must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the
purchase date. Receipts are mandatory for any single item over 25 dollars. Categories
include travel, meals during travel, professional development, and approved software
subscriptions. Meals without travel are reimbursable only for client-facing events and
must list the attendees. Expenses submitted after 90 days are rejected by policy and
cannot be appealed. Reimbursements are paid with the next regular payroll cycle after
approval, typically within two weeks.

Section 7: Security Training and Compliance

All employees must complete quarterly security training assigned by the IT department.
The training covers phishing recognition, password hygiene, and handling of customer data.
Completion is mandatory within 14 days of assignment; uncompleted assignments escalate to
the employee's manager after that window and to the department head after 30 days.
Employees in engineering and support roles additionally complete an annual secure coding
review. Failure to complete required training within 60 days may affect eligibility for
the annual bonus, as described in the compensation plan. The security team runs unannounced
phishing simulations each quarter; employees who click a simulation link are enrolled in a
short remedial module rather than disciplined, unless the incident involves a credential
being entered on a known-malicious site.

Section 8: Confidentiality

Employees must not share internal documentation, credentials, or customer data outside the
company. Confidential documents are labeled with the Acme Confidential watermark. Public
discussion of roadmap items is prohibited until the official announcement date published by
the communications team.
